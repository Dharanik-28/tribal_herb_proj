About TribalHerb

🌿 Our Purpose

TribalHerb is designed to explore the rich medicinal plant heritage of Araku, Chintapalli and Paderu tribal regions. The project presents information about plants, traditional knowledge and Ayurvedic practices in a simple and accessible way.

🔎 What You Can Discover

Explore different plants, their botanical names, traditional uses, plant parts used, images, local names and information about their presence in tribal regions.

🌱 What is Ayurveda?

Ayurveda is a traditional system of health knowledge that originated in India. It focuses on maintaining balance between the body, mind and lifestyle through traditional practices, diet, herbs and daily routines.

🍃 Ayurvedic Plants

Many plants are traditionally used in Ayurveda. Examples include Tulsi, Turmeric, Amla, Neem, Ashwagandha and Aloe Vera. Different parts of plants such as roots, leaves, fruits, seeds and rhizomes may be used in traditional preparations.

🌿 Traditional Uses

Ayurvedic traditions describe plants for various traditional purposes such as supporting digestion, skincare, general wellness and maintaining healthy daily routines. Traditional use does not necessarily mean that a plant is proven to treat a medical condition.

🌱 Parts of Plants Used

Different plants may traditionally use different parts, including roots, leaves, flowers, fruits, seeds, bark and rhizomes. For example, turmeric commonly refers to the rhizome, while Amla is traditionally valued for its fruit.

🏞️ Tribal Traditional Knowledge

Tribal communities have developed knowledge about local plants and their traditional uses over generations. TribalHerb aims to present such knowledge respectfully while recognizing the importance of protecting cultural knowledge and biodiversity.

🌍 Sustainable Herbal Resources

Sustainable collection and cultivation of medicinal plants can help protect biodiversity and support local livelihoods. Responsible harvesting is important so that valuable plant species can continue to grow for future generations.

✅ Verified Information

TribalHerb can combine botanical information from reliable biodiversity databases with traditional knowledge sources. Information should be checked against authoritative references before being used for health or medical decisions.

⚠️ Important Information

The Ayurvedic information presented on this website is intended for educational purposes. Herbal products and plants can have side effects or interact with medicines. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before using herbs for a medical condition.
