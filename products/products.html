🏞️ Tribal Herbal Products

Tribal communities in forest regions may collect, cultivate and process locally available plant resources. Examples of products can include herbal powders, oils, teas, spices, soaps and other plant-based products.

Responsible sourcing, sustainable cultivation and respect for traditional knowledge are important when developing herbal products.
